HOW IT WORKS

Three moves. Two income streams.

Clip the best moments from big streams with AI, post them to TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and earn from clipping campaigns plus YouTube.

STEP 1

Set up your clip pages

Make one page per platform, all with the same name, e.g. GTA Daily Clips. No face, no gameplay of your own.

  1. Create a TikTok account, an Instagram account (switch it to a Creator account) and a YouTube channel.
  2. Same name, same profile picture, bio: "Best GTA 6 moments, daily 🎮".
  3. Keep every account public, or campaigns can't count your views.
STEP 2

Join paid clipping campaigns

Streamers and brands post a budget and pay clippers for every 1,000 views. Most campaigns pay around $1 per 1,000 views.

STEP 7

YouTube pays you on top

Keep posting to your YouTube Shorts page. Once it grows, YouTube shares ad revenue with you too.

  • Fan funding: 500 subscribers + 3M Shorts views in 90 days.
  • Ad revenue: 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days (or 4,000 watch hours).
  • From 1 Feb 2027 new channels need 20M Shorts views (or 8,000 hours), so start early.
YOUR DAILY ROUTINE

One hour a day

  1. 10 min: check campaigns for new GTA 6 streams.
  2. 20 min: run 2–3 streams through the AI clipper.
  3. 15 min: pick the best clips, fix the hooks.
  4. 15 min: schedule in Buffer and submit links.
Clip streamers who run campaigns or allow clipping, add your own captions and edits, and skip pure story cutscenes or ending spoilers. That keeps your pages safe.
